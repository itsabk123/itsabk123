# 09 · Launch, acquisition & KPIs

## Phased launch
1. **Founding supply (weeks 0–6):** hand-recruit podcasts and creators in 2 niches (finance/real-estate, B2B/tech; Arabic + English). Offer: free verified profile, media-kit-ready profile page, featured placement window (promotion records).
2. **Concierge demand (weeks 4–10):** 10–20 brands via direct B2B outreach and agency partners; run their first campaigns with assisted matchmaking; first campaign fee-free promotion.
3. **Open self-serve:** once completion rate and repeat rate are healthy in the pilot niches, expand niches using Marketplace health signals.

## Growth loops (built hooks)
- Every profile and campaign has a shareable, indexable URL with OG/JSON-LD (only substantive profiles go in the sitemap).
- Referral links on the dashboard; rewards qualify on the referee's **first completed collaboration**.
- Completed deals prompt reviews and "Rebook"; CRM moves to Repeat client.
- Saved searches with daily-max alerts; stale-proposal nudges; unfinished-profile nudges; permit-expiry reminders.
- Agencies bring rosters (consent-based representation) and are the legal route for visitor permits.

## Content/SEO plan
Editorial via Admin → Content: podcast monetisation in the UAE, permit how-to (with official links), measuring creator campaigns, Arabic podcast landscape, case studies only from real deals with permission. Programmatic pages only where real inventory exists (e.g. `/discover/podcasts?emirate=Dubai&industry=…` canonicalised to the base path; no thin generated pages).

## KPI framework (computed in admin; targets set from observed data, not assumed)
Acquisition: registrations by referral/channel · Activation: profile published, first proposal, first response · Liquidity: invite acceptance, application rate, time to agreement, unanswered >3 days, supply/demand by segment · Financial: GMV, commission, average deal value, MRR · Retention: repeat partner pairs, 30/90-day active · Trust: dispute rate, refund rate, review averages.
